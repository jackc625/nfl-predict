How should the This Week page be laid out?

All three use the Broadcast style and the model's real Week 3 numbers; market lines and the 3 bets are examples, and two games show the "no line yet" state. Note the one-line disclaimer with a "Why?" link under the header -- that's the condensed honesty-label pattern you picked. Hover cards/rows.

L1. Grouped by TV window

Score-bug cards in a 3-column grid, grouped under broadcast-style tags (Sunday 1:00, Sunday 4:05/4:25, Sunday Night, Monday Night). Cards on the bet list get a yellow top edge and a BET flag. Closest to today's page, just reorganized.

L2. The Board

One row per game, like a sportsbook board: win probability, model spread vs line, model total vs line, and the bet chip, all in columns. The whole slate fits on one screen and is easy to scan down a column. Least visual, most information-dense.

L3. Headliners + slate

The week's bets get big featured cards at the top (the pick in large yellow type, stake, edge), then every game below as a smaller 4-column grid. Answers "what should I look at?" first. The /bets page would still hold the full detail.
