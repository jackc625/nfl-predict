Methodology Notes

Walk-Forward Validation

This report uses strict walk-forward temporal validation with no future data leakage. For each holdout season Y, the model is trained on seasons [{{ metrics.config.first_data_season }}..Y-2] with hyperparameter validation on season Y-1. This rolling approach ensures realistic out-of-sample evaluation.

Era Normalization

The NFL expanded from 16 to 17 regular-season games starting in 2021. Week numbers are normalized to season progress (0-1 scale) so that cross-era comparisons are fair.

Betting Simulation Methodology

  • Half-point slippage applied to all ATS and O/U bets (moves against bettor)
  • WP bets use moneyline directly (no slippage)
  • Standard -110 vig assumed for ATS/O/U bets
  • Quarter-Kelly (25% of full Kelly) for dynamic sizing strategy
  • Minimum edge threshold required before placing any bet
